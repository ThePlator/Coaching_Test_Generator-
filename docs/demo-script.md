# Demo Video Script (1–2 min)

**Goal:** chapter in → printed test out, with proof that the answers come from the chapter.
**Record:** screen at 1080p plus your voice. Speed up the generation wait to 4× in editing. End on a photo of the printed paper.

**Before recording**

- Run `scripts/preflight.py --warm` so the model is loaded.
- Set the institute name in the sidebar.
- Use `samples/sample_chapter.txt`, or the teacher's chapter if they allow it.
- Close notifications, zoom the browser to 110%.

| # | Time | Screen | Say (roughly) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0:00–0:10 | Title card: "Coaching Test Generator · प्रश्न पत्र", then a photo of a handwritten test or a coaching classroom | "Coaching teachers in Patna spend hours every week writing and translating practice tests." |
| 2 | 0:10–0:20 | App home with the green 🟢 Gemma light. Turn Wi-Fi off on camera. | "This runs Gemma on the teacher's own laptop. No internet, no per-question bill, and their notes stay private." |
| 3 | 0:20–0:30 | Upload the PDF or photo, then the "4 pages, 3 parts" message | "Upload a chapter as a PDF, your notes, or even photos of the book." |
| 4 | 0:30–0:40 | Settings: 10 questions, MCQ, medium, **Bilingual**, then Generate. Progress bar at 4×. | "Choose how many questions, and Hindi, English, or both on one sheet." |
| 5 | 0:40–1:05 | Review: zoom on one card showing the ✅ answer and the 📖 source line. Edit one option. Regenerate one. | "Every question shows the exact line from the chapter that proves the answer. If Gemma can't point to one, the question is dropped. The teacher can edit or regenerate anything, and nothing prints until they've reviewed it." |
| 6 | 1:05–1:20 | Tick reviewed, Create PDFs. Open the student paper (bilingual header, क/ख options), then the answer key with source lines. | "Two PDFs: the student paper and an answer key the teacher can verify in seconds." |
| 7 | 1:20–1:35 | Photo of the printed paper from the session (no student names), plus a caption with the real numbers | "[Teacher] made this 10-question test from their own chapter in [time]. Normally it takes them [their answer]." |
| 8 | 1:35–1:45 | End card: GitHub URL, "MIT · Gemma via Ollama · ₹199/month idea" | "It's open source. Link below." |

**Captions:** burn in English subtitles, and add Hindi for the opening line if you have time.
