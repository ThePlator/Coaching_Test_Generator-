Every question is written from, and checked against, your chapter

A wrong answer key costs a teacher credibility in front of students. So accuracy comes before quantity.

Add your chapter

PDF, pasted notes, or phone photos of the book. Gemma reads the photos.

Pick your test

5–50 questions: MCQ, true/false, fill in the blank, short answer. Easy to hard. हिंदी, English or both.

Questions are checked

Each answer must quote a real line from your chapter. Made-up or repeated questions are dropped.

Review, then print

Edit, delete or regenerate anything. Then print the student paper and an answer key with source lines.

Why it costs so little

No per-question bill

It runs Google's open Gemma model on the laptop you already have. There's no cloud AI to pay for every time you make a test.

Your notes stay yours

Chapters and notes never leave your computer. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.

Power cut? Bad network?

Once it's set up, it works without internet.

Real exam Hindi

Questions are written directly in standard exam Hindi, not word-for-word translation, and print correctly with proper Devanagari fonts.
